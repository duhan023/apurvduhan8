Selected Work

Built, shipped,
and documented.

Three pieces of delivery worth reading about in full.

Why

Every project runs on a RAID log — risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies — and most of them die in a messy spreadsheet with no history and no owner.

How

I wrote three documents before a line of code existed: a plain-language spec anyone could approve, a build plan an AI coding agent could follow without guessing, and a constitution of non-negotiables — locked audit history, tests first, privacy by default, accessibility throughout. Then the tool was built to those documents, with every screen tracing back to a numbered requirement.

What it shows

That I manage AI-driven delivery the way I manage any program: define the what, control the how, and make every change traceable.

Read the documents
Problem

A high-stakes portal migration across delinquent, cured and paid-off loan segments — regulatory exposure, no tolerable downtime, and a fixed date.

Action

End-to-end ownership: a structured RAID register and dependency matrix, UAT gates, sprint-level quality checkpoints, and weekly data-backed status straight to senior technology leadership. 160+ user stories written with full acceptance criteria to kill rework before it started.

Result

Delivered on schedule and within budget with a zero-defect launch. Customer-impacting defects fell 32%, post-launch escalations fell 22%, and the audit trail held up.

Tools

JIRA · Confluence · Agile / Scrum · RAID · UAT gates · Power BI

Problem

A multi-region supply chain with slow turnaround and almost no visibility for the people making the decisions.

Action

Root-cause analysis across 18 monthly shipment cycles, SOP standardization, vendor escalation protocols, and Power BI dashboards tracking on-time delivery, order accuracy, backlog and vendor performance — while running six concurrent capital import work streams.
