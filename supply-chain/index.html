Data note

The synthetic dataset models four operating regions, 18 monthly cycles, 120+ SKUs, six concurrent workstreams, and a simulated vendor network. It illustrates resume-verified outcomes of 18–20% turnaround improvement and 96% compliance. Individual records, vendor identities, and chart-level values are fictional.

About this exhibit

What is real, and what is made.

The programme was real: eighteen monthly shipment cycles across Germany, Brazil, Europe and Asia-Pacific, fifty-plus vendors and customs partners, a hundred and twenty active SKUs, six capital-import work streams, and a Power BI dashboard leadership actually opened. The outcomes on the record are real: turnaround down 18–20%, supply continuity sustained at 96%.

Everything on this page is generated. A seeded script produces 24 fictional vendors, their monthly orders in each region, late-shipment causes, backlog age and continuity, with an intervention in August 2022 and a response curve after it. The generator is tuned so the headline outcomes match the record. No company data left the company.
